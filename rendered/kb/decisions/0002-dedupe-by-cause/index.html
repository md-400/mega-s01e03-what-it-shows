Two fixes, two tickets

Shared parser

Track the parser cause in GitHub issue 1. For a day-first tenant, the shared parser reads the month before the day. Report R-41200 describes 7 April becoming 4 July for Harbour Dental (en-GB, app). Report R-41205 is also a parser report despite its intake hint tag and en-US browser locale: Morrow Tax’s tenant locale is fr-FR.

The fix must respect the tenant’s intended date format through the shared parsing path. Test ambiguous dates in both directions, unambiguous days above 12, and every affected channel. Retain the one-day descriptions as regression evidence without claiming an unproved separate defect.

Booking-v3 field hint

Track the hint cause in GitHub issue 2. For day-first tenants on booking-v3, the field says MM/DD/YYYY while the form reads dates day-first. People follow that hint and the form interprets their input in the opposite order. Report R-41230 is Copper Studio’s booking-v3 form (es-ES), even though intake tagged it parser. R-41241 is the same cause for Alder Physio (de-DE); the browser locale does not change the tenant.

Align the visible hint and form validation with the tenant’s date format. Test booking-v3 for Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour, and ensure booking-v2 remains correct. Fixing the shared parser alone does not fix this misleading instruction.

The queue is not the incident clock

Read received and merged timestamps separately. Parser reports begin on 1 April; hint reports begin on 17 April. The large merge spike in the week of 29 June is backlog processing, not the first occurrence or a surge in newly received reports. Reports continue after that merge, through the export’s last received date in October. Neither a merge nor the word “known” proves a fix shipped.

How the desk handles the next report

  1. Deduplicate export rows by report ID. Read the subject and body, then look up the product, tenant locale, booking form version and channel; browser locale and intake tags are only clues.
  2. For this booking system, use the tenant-aware cause rule in causes.ts: a day-first tenant on a booking-v3 form belongs to the hint cause; other affected paths belong to parser.
  3. Merge only when the cause and fix match an existing ticket. Different subjects can share a cause; identical subjects can hide different causes. A missing tenant or unplaced cause needs investigation, not a subject-only merge. Never default to merging when unsure.
  4. Keep one ticket per confirmed cause and keep its report count and evidence current. When new reports arrive, add their data, rerun the analysis and render, then update the matching ticket. Do not close a newly different problem as a duplicate of #4471 just because it mentions a date.
  5. Require evidence that the relevant fix shipped and that its regression tests pass before marking it fixed. A known status or queued fix does not stop the desk from triaging continuing reports.
